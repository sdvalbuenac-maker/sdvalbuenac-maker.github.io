About

The story of Visual Rocket Flyer

A small, independent Latin American studio making games with identity.

Our mission

Making games that feel like home. We love seeing a Venezuelan in Santiago, Lima, Madrid or São Paulo laugh at a word they haven’t heard in years, or a grandma and her grandkid arguing over who has more street smarts.

How we work

We care about every detail: animations, music, voices and celebrations. We test every version with real players before launch and listen to what they tell us. Our games are free and supported by optional, respectful ads.

Our alien

The brand mascot arrived from another galaxy, fell in love with the beach, Latin music and food, and decided to stay and code with us. You'll see him on our social media, on this site and in what's coming next.
