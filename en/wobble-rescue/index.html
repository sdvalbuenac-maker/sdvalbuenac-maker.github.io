Features

What's inside Wobble Rescue?

🌍

8 worlds

Meadow, Wood Workshop, Spring Factory, Balloon Sky, Magnet Mine, Portal Dimension, Confetti Volcano and the Castle.

🧩

120 levels

15 levels per world, each with a boss level and stars to collect.

⚙️

Real physics

Balls, levers, springs, conveyor belts, balloons, magnets, portals and confetti bombs that behave like the real thing.

🎈

Bloop and friends

Bloop, Pip, Lumi and Zig versus Gruñubarrón, a grumpy grey cloud who wants to ruin the party.

🌐

In three languages

Spanish, English and Portuguese.

🎉

The Big Party

In the end, even the villain joins the celebration.

The story

Gruñubarrón, a grey and grumpy cloud, kidnapped the Wobblies to ruin the big party. Bloop, a very brave blue jelly, must use wits and physics to rescue them all.
