Features

What's inside RETRUCOO!?

🃏

Venezuelan rules

With vira, perico (the 11) and perica (the 10), envido, flor and games to 24 in two rounds of 12.

🗣️

Talking opponents

Play against characters with Venezuelan accents who sing, tease and shout their plays.

🌐

Online

Set up a table and play with your friends, one-on-one or in pairs.

🎨

Classic deck

Traditional public-domain Spanish cards with our own card back.

👤

Your profile

Avatar, voice, coins, shop, missions and a daily gift.

😂

Reactions

Laughs, teasing and table slams with vibration to spice up the game.
