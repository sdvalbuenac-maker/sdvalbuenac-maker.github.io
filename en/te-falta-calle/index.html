How to play

Each level shows a word or idea in neutral Spanish. Your mission is to write how they say it in Venezuela. The letters appear on the asphalt and you complete the word. Get it wrong and Lorenzo the Parrot will let you know with a "¡Te falta calle!!" stamp.

If you get stuck, use Help: reveal letters with coins or watch a short ad to earn more. You can also share the word with your friends so they can help, and the first share of each day gives you 30 free coins.

Who it's for

For Venezuelans at home and abroad who want to laugh and reminisce, and for anyone who wants to learn to talk like a local. It's made for ages 13+ and the whole family.

Web and iPhone version

The web version is the same game as on Android. Your progress (coins, levels and settings) is saved in your phone’s browser. On iPhone we recommend adding it to your Home Screen: it opens full screen like an app and your progress stays safe.

FAQ
Is it free?

Yes, completely. The game is supported by short, optional ads.

Is there an iPhone version?

Yes: the web version works in Safari and you can install it on your Home Screen. See the guide on the "Play on iPhone" page.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. If you clear your browser data or uninstall the app, your progress is lost. On iPhone, install it on your Home Screen so Safari doesn’t erase it.

Does my Android progress carry over to iPhone?

Not yet: each device keeps its own progress.

Do I need to sign up?

No. We don't ask for your name, email or a password.
