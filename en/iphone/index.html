iPhone guide

Play on iPhone like an app

Te falta calle!! works in Safari. Add it to your Home Screen and it opens full screen like any app, with your progress saved.

Open the game

1. Open in Safari

On your iPhone, open this page in Safari (not inside TikTok or Instagram) and tap Open the game. If you came from another app, tap the three dots and choose "Open in Safari".

2. Tap Share

With the game open, tap the Share button: the little square with the arrow pointing up, at the bottom or top of the screen.

3. Add to Home Screen

Scroll and tap "Add to Home Screen", then Add. The Te falta calle!! icon will appear next to your apps.

Common questions

Why isn't it on the App Store? For now we're releasing the web version so you can play right away, without waiting. It's the same game as on Android.

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your iPhone. Always open it from the Home Screen icon: Safari may delete data from pages that aren't on the Home Screen and go unused for several days.

Does it use a lot of data? The first time it downloads the game (a few MB). After that it loads faster.

Does it work on iPad or a computer? Yes. On a computer it shows inside a phone-shaped frame.
