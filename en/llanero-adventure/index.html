Llanero Adventure

Run, jump and travel Venezuela with the Llanero, the Sifrina and the Urbano. Collect mangoes and arepas, dodge the villains and celebrate every finish line with a llanero band.

Features

What's inside Llanero Adventure?

🏞️

15 destinations

From the plains to the beach, from the mountains to the city: every level is a corner of Venezuela full of life.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑

Three characters

Pick the Llanero, the Sifrina or the Urbano. Each with their own style.

🥭

Mangoes and chicha

Collect mangoes, drink a chicha to survive a hit and use the double jump.

🏍️

Motorbike bonus

Find the cuatro hidden in the surprise blocks and ride a motorbike to a track in the clouds full of arepas.

🎶

Llanero music

Harp, cuatro and maracas: the llanero band sings every time you reach the finish.

🐒

Villains

Face the Monkey King and other characters who won't make it easy.

About the game

Llanero Adventure is a 2D platformer for Android inspired by the landscapes, food and music of Venezuela. It’s free and works offline.

What about iPhone or the browser?

We're preparing the web version so iPhone users can enjoy it too. Follow us on TikTok to know when it's out.

FAQ
Is it free?

Yes. It has optional ads that give you rewards, like continuing or reviving.

Does it work offline?

Yes, you can play without a connection.
