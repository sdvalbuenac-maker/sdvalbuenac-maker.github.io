Te falta calle!!

Do you know how Venezuelans say "problem"? Or "party"? If you hesitated… te falta calle! (you need more street smarts!). Spell the word letter by letter, level up with Grandma Carmen, Compadre Chuy and Chamo Yeico, and earn your street ID card.

  • 360+ words and trivia
  • Travel Venezuela on the map
  • Animated street parties
  • Family mode: grandma vs. grandkid
  • Share and earn coins
  • Your progress is saved
