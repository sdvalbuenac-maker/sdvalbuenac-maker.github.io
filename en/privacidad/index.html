Privacy policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy explains what data is used on the website visualrocketflyers.com and in the games by Visual Rocket Flyer Games (Te falta calle!!, Llanero Adventure, Wobble Rescue and RETRUCOO!), both in their mobile and web versions.

1. Data the games store

  • Progress and settings (levels, coins, stars, language, sound): stored on your device or in your browser’s storage. We have no user accounts and we don’t receive that progress.
  • Age: some games ask once for your birth year to show suitable ads. It is stored only on your device.
  • RETRUCOO! online mode: uses an anonymous identifier and the nickname you choose to set up tables, through Unity Gaming Services. We don’t ask for email or phone number.

We don't ask for your name, email, phone number, precise location or photos.

2. Advertising

The apps use Google AdMob and the website and web versions may use Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies, your device’s advertising ID, your IP address (for approximate location), technical device data and your interaction with ads to show, measure and personalize them.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings or at aboutads.info. More information: how Google uses advertising data.

Where required by law (for example in the European Union, the United Kingdom or Brazil), we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads.

3. Cookies and browser storage

The website uses your browser’s local storage so the web games can save your progress. Advertising cookies are set by Google and its partners, as explained above. You can clear cookies and site data in your browser settings (this also erases your progress in the web games).

4. Children

Our games are for a general audience. If a player indicates they are under 13, only child-appropriate, non-personalized ads are requested.

5. Security

The website uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Advertising data is sent to Google encrypted.

6. Your choices

  • Delete your progress from each game’s settings, by uninstalling the app or by clearing the site data in your browser.
  • Reset or limit your advertising ID in your Android or iOS settings.
  • Write to us with any question about your data.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version at this same address.
